# Changes from upstream

Everything below was added or changed in this fork (by liamhatherton), compiled from the commit history.

## Sci-fi setting and data model

1. **Factions renamed to Groups.** The tab, folder (`Groups`), "New Group" command and modal, and the character `group` field all replace the old faction wording.
2. **"Magic" lore category renamed to "Tech".**
3. **New location types:** planet, dwarf planet, moon, station, asteroid, belt and ship, alongside the original city/region/building/landmark/other.
4. **Optional Ship and Home fields on characters.** Both are in the New Character form and written to the note's frontmatter.
5. **Group types.** Groups get a Type dropdown (Corporation, Government, Military, Criminal), saved to frontmatter and shown in the note body.
6. **New character note template.** Colored section headings (Origin, Physical Description, Occupation, Resume, Role In Story, Goals, Personality, Habits/Mannerisms, Earlier Life, Internal Conflicts, External Conflicts). Text entered in the form goes under its heading and the rest are left empty to fill in later. The Secrets field was removed.

## Sidebar cards

7. **Character portraits.** Each character card shows a thumbnail of the first image embedded in the note (wiki or markdown embeds, local files or image URLs). Portrait and border sizes were adjusted afterwards.
8. **Thumbnails for Locations and Groups**, the same way as characters.
9. **Clearer character card details.** Two labelled lines, "Age · Home" then "Group · Ship". Empty values are left out and the role badge sits on its own line.
10. **Expandable cards.** Clicking a card expands a preview of the note inside the sidebar instead of opening it. The expanded card floats over the list (with a short animation), filling the sidebar from just under the section tabs to the bottom with a 20px margin (38px at the bottom, clear of a status bar); the section header, search bar and list behind it are dimmed and can't be used, while the tabs stay clickable. Only one entry is expanded at a time: expanding another (for example by clicking a `[[link]]` in the preview, or with Back / Forward) closes the open one and shows the new one straight away, without the animation. The floating card has a soft border in the accent color (30% opacity). Its arrow turns into an X while it floats, and clicking its title row shrinks it back into its place in the list. Text in the preview can be highlighted and copied (Ctrl/Cmd+C, or right-click the selection for **Copy**). The preview leaves out frontmatter, the portrait image (already the card's thumbnail) and the repeated title heading; other images show as 64px cropped squares that open full-screen when clicked, and has a Modify MD button to open the note (plus an inline Edit toggle, see 27).
11. **Frontmatter values with quotes are read correctly.** Quoted values (`"..."` / `'...'`) are unquoted before they are displayed.

## Organization and ordering

12. **Characters grouped by group.** Each group gets a collapsible section, sorted alphabetically with "No Group" last. Collapsed state is remembered.
13. **Group logos on character group headers.** The logo is the first image in the matching group note.
14. **Drag-and-drop ordering.** Cards can be dragged into a custom order. This started with characters (within their group group) and now covers every tab. The order is saved in plugin data (notes are never modified) and still applies after a note is renamed.
15. **Hierarchical Locations tab.** Locations nest under their `parent` location (plain names and `[[wiki links]]` both work, and cycles are ignored). Each level can be reordered separately, and dragging a parent moves its whole subtree with it.
16. **Collapsible location tree.** Every location has a collapsible label, and collapsing a parent hides its subtree. Nested labels keep their own saved state.
17. **Ships section.** Ship-type locations are never nested because they move around. They get their own collapsible Ships section at the bottom of Locations.
18. **Groups grouped by type** into collapsible Corporation / Government / Military / Criminal / Unassigned sections, each with its own drag-to-reorder list. An group with a `subsidiary-of` property (set from the New Group form, or by hand; plain names and `[[wiki links]]` both work) is shown under its parent's card in a collapsible **Subsidiaries** label instead of its own type section. If the parent can't be found, it stays in its type section.

## Navigation and search

19. **Fixed sidebar header.** The title, tabs, section header and search bar stay put while the list below scrolls, with a shadow when the list is scrolled. Scroll position is kept across redraws.
20. **Improved search.** The search bar sits under each tab's section header, and each tab keeps its own query. Every word typed must match, ignoring case and accents. Characters match on name, group, ship and home, and the other tabs on the name plus the note text. Non-matching cards and empty groups are hidden, a "No … match" line appears when nothing matches, and Esc or the clear button resets the search. On Locations, the parents of a matching entry stay visible.
21. **Back/Forward navigation.** A history of tab switches and expanded cards is kept. An expanded entry's toolbar starts with Back and Forward arrows (then a faint divider and the Bookmark button) that step to the entry expanded before or after it, such as the one you came from after clicking a `[[link]]`; they're greyed out when there's nowhere to go. The section header's own Back and Forward buttons, which also stepped through tab switches, are hidden for now (a floating card covers the section header); set `SHOW_NAV_BUTTONS` to `true` in `src/main.ts` to bring them back.
22. **Wiki links inside the sidebar.** Clicking a `[[link]]` in an expanded preview jumps to that entry's card: it switches tab, opens collapsed groups, clears a hiding search, then expands and scrolls to the card. Links outside the Universe folder open normally.
23. **Reload button** in the section header, to redraw the sidebar on demand. The section header no longer repeats the current section's name (the highlighted tab already shows it).
24. **Bookmarks.** Every expanded entry has a **Bookmark** button on the left of its toolbar, after Back / Forward (opposite Modify MD / Edit); it turns the accent color when the entry is bookmarked. A **Bookmarks** button (icon and label) to the right of the sidebar's title, the same size as Reload and + New, swaps the list for a Bookmarks view, with bookmarked entries grouped by section (collapsible and drag-to-reorder). Clicking the button again returns to the last section. Bookmarks are kept in plugin data and follow renames.
25. **Click-to-zoom photos.** When an entry with a photo is expanded (in any section tab or Bookmarks), hovering the photo shows a magnifier badge in its top-right corner, and clicking it opens the image full-screen. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, and click or press Esc to close, with no need to open the note first.
26. **Double-click to collapse a section.** Double-clicking the tab you're already on (Characters, Locations, Groups, Lore or Timeline) closes the expanded (floating) card and folds every collapsible group in that section. The folded state is saved like a manual fold.
27. **Inline editing in the sidebar.** An expanded entry's toolbar, at the top of the expanded area above the note's text, has Modify MD (opens the note in the main editor) and Edit. Edit replaces the preview with Obsidian's own Live Preview editor for the note's text (formatting shown as you type, `[[link]]` suggestions, editor hotkeys such as bold), with the note's properties in a small raw YAML **Properties** box above it, collapsed until you click its header. Every image in the note shows in the editor except the portrait, and the `# Title` heading line that repeats the card's title is hidden too; both are kept out of the editor and put back at the top of the note on save (when they sit at the top of the body, where the templates put them). Editing happens in the floating card (see 10); clicking its X while editing asks first if there are unsaved changes. The editor scrolls inside the card, so Cancel and Save stay in view, and saving or cancelling returns to the floating preview. The buttons become Cancel and Save. Nothing is written to the file until you Save, which updates the note and returns to the preview, with the card's name, badges and grouping updated. Cancel discards the changes (asking first if anything changed). Ctrl/Cmd+S or Ctrl/Cmd+Enter also save, Esc cancels, and collapsing a card with unsaved edits asks first. If the note was changed elsewhere while you were editing, saving asks before overwriting it. The Live Preview editor relies on an undocumented Obsidian API (see [Undocumented Obsidian API](UNDOCUMENTED-API.md)); the **Sidebar editor** setting switches to a plain raw-markdown text box instead.
28. **Drag an image onto a card to set its portrait.** Drop an image file (e.g. from File Explorer) onto any Characters, Locations, Groups, Lore or Timeline card, collapsed or expanded; the card is outlined while the image is over it. If the entry already has a portrait, you're asked whether to replace it: No changes nothing, Yes swaps that image's embed for the new one in place (keeping any size such as `|300`). If it has no portrait, the image is embedded at the very top of the note, right after its properties and before the title heading. Images from outside the vault are copied into a per-section folder under `Images` in your Universe folder (e.g. `UniverseBuilder/Images/Characters/`), so notes never link to files outside the vault and portraits travel with it through any sync method; images already in the vault are linked where they are.
29. **Portrait drop area on every New entry form.** New Character, Location, Group, Lore Entry and Timeline Event forms open with a drop area above the fields: drag an image onto it (or click it to choose a file, which also works on mobile) and it's previewed right away. When you click Create, an image from outside the vault is copied into that section's folder under `Images` in your Universe folder (e.g. `UniverseBuilder/Images/Locations/`) and embedded at the very top of the new note, right after its properties, so it becomes the entry's portrait. The file keeps its name; a number is added if a different image in that folder already has that name, and an image imported there before is reused rather than copied again. Cancelling the form imports nothing. This also gives Lore and Timeline cards portraits, the same as the other tabs. The same drop area appears above the inline editor (item 27) of an entry that has no portrait yet; the image is imported when you Save, and Cancel imports nothing.
30. **Delete an entry from the sidebar.** Below an expanded entry's text (or its inline editor) is a divider and a red **DELETE** button on the right, the same size as the toolbar buttons at the top. It asks first ("Are you sure you want to delete this Character entry, Fisty?", naming the entry's section and its name or title); confirming closes the card and moves the note to the trash, following Obsidian's **Deleted files** setting so it can be restored. The entry also drops out of Bookmarks and the Back / Forward history.

## Languages

31. **Ten languages.** Everything the plugin shows (sidebar, New entry forms, notices, dialogs, command names and settings) is translated into English, Spanish, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese and Japanese, with one file per language in `src/locales/`. The language follows Obsidian's own (read with `getLanguage()`; an exact match such as `pt-BR` first, then the base language, so `fr-CA` uses French and `zh-TW` uses Simplified Chinese), falling back to English, and the **Language** setting overrides it. Changing it redraws the sidebar, renames the commands and rebuilds the settings tab straight away. New notes are written with headings and labels in the language in use when they're created. Folder names (`Characters`, `Locations`, `Groups`, `Lore`, `Timeline`, `Images`), frontmatter keys and stored values (`role: protagonist`, `type: ship`, ...) always stay in English so switching language never breaks a vault; known values are only translated where they're shown, such as a card's badge.
